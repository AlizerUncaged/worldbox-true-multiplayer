# Playing Together

Open the multiplayer window from the **True Multiplayer** tab or with **F9**.

## Account

Pick a username (3 to 24 letters, digits, `_`, `-` or `.`) and a password, then press
**Register**. Next time, **Log in**.

**Keep me signed in** is on by default: the game signs you back in on every launch, and if
your connection drops it reconnects, signs in and puts you back in your room by itself.
**Log out** forgets the saved login on this PC.

## Lobbies and rooms

The window lists the server's **lobbies** (always open) and everyone's **public rooms**,
with player counts. The list refreshes on its own.

- **Join** a lobby or public room with one click. Rooms marked **Locked** ask for a password.
- **Create a room**: give it a name, an optional password (up to 16 characters), and choose
  **Public** (listed) or **Unlisted** (join by code only).
- **Join by code**: every room has a 4-character code. Inside a room, **Copy code** puts it
  on your clipboard to share.

> **Joining replaces your current world** with the room's world. Save your own world first
> if you want to keep it.

## Roles

| Role | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Holds the world. Their game runs the real simulation; everyone else plays inside it. Can promote and demote admins and kick anyone. |
| **Admin** | Can kick players (not other admins or the Owner). |
| **Player** | Plays in the Owner's world. |

The first player into an empty lobby becomes its Owner with their current world. If the
Owner leaves, an admin (or the longest-present player) becomes the new Owner and the world
carries on.

## In the room

- Everyone's **god hand** shows on your map with their name. Players outside your view are
  pinned to the edge of the screen, dimmed.
- God powers, spawns, terrain edits, disasters and everything they cause are mirrored to
  every player.
- **Resync** (players) asks the Owner to send the world again if something looks off.
  **Resend world** (Owner) pushes the world to everyone.
- Announcements from the server's admins appear on screen.

## Using another server

Click **Server settings...** (on the login screen or in the toolbar when offline), enter the
host and port, and press **Save & reconnect**. **Use the default server** goes back to the
official one.
